# ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-07-30

## Decision

Architectural decisions are recorded as short documents in `adr/`, numbered sequentially, never edited after acceptance (superseded by new ADRs instead). There is no formal RFC process at this scale; ADRs double as durable context for both human and AI contributors.

## Format

Status line, Decision, Context, Consequences. One page maximum. An ADR that needs more than a page is two decisions.
